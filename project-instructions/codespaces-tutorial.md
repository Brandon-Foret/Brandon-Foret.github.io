# Welcome to Coding in Codespaces

Welcome. You are now coding in a cloud-powered VS Code environment called a **Codespace**.

This setup gives you everything you need for completing projects in this class without extra installs on your computer. If the editor looks unfamiliar, no worries. You only need a few controls to get started.

## Quick Tour: Hide Panels for Easier Coding

VS Code has two areas you will use all the time:

1. **Sidebar** (usually on the left): shows your files.
2. **Panel** (usually at the bottom): shows terminal output and other tools.

**However**, the default codespace setup has several panels and side panels open, and you may feel a bit cramped by the layout on your screen.

There are three black and white buttons at the very top right of this tab that will toggle the different panels open/closed. We recommend clicking the one on the right and the one in the middle to close the right side panel and the bottom panel for the majority of time. You can always click on the button again to show the panel that was closed.

## Open Files in Split Screen

For your first project, several files may already be open when the Codespace loads.

To set up split view:

1. Find `index.html` in the open tabs.
2. Click and drag the `index.html` tab to the right side of the editor.
3. Release when you see the right-side highlight.
4. Keep your other file open on the left.

Now you can view two files at the same time, which is helpful for following instructions while coding.
